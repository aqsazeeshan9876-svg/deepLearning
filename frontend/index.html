Curriculum Scope & Constraints (DL-024)

This system is an academic accessibility research prototype specifically evaluated on discrete dynamic gesture sequences sampled from short clips. Continuous unsegmented signing, grammatical inflections, facial affect tracking, and real-time sentence construction are intentionally designated as out of scope according to the project evaluation criteria.
